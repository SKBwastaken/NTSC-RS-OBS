Compatibility, stability and performance have not been fully tested.

This is an unofficial native NTSC/VHS filter for OBS Studio on Windows.
It is CPU-based, supports SDR only, and adds video latency without delaying audio.
Tested with OBS 32.2.2 on Windows.

Close OBS before installation. Setup never closes OBS automatically.
The installer and plugin are not code-signed.
There is no automatic updater, telemetry, or network download in this installer.
